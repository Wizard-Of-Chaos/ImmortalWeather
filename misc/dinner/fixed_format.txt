Chili|Ingredients|1 lb beef stew meat (the type you can get like five pounds of for a quarter)|14 oz beef broth (roughly the size of a beef broth container)|1 can dark red kidney beans (you can cook these yourself but I never bother)|3 tbsp olive oil|Flour|**Spice mix:**|2 tsp oregano|1 tsp salt|3 tbsp chili powder|1 tbsp cumin|2 cloves garlic, chopped (or the equivalent in powder)|Equipment|Large pot|Method|Mince up the garlic and mix all your spices together.|In a plastic bag, cover the beef chunks in flour.|Heat up the oil in the pot. It is safe to cook with when you spit in it and it sizzles properly.|Shake off the excess flour from the beef chunks and dump them in the pot. Sear until they’re browned. You don’t need to be too thorough.|Throw in the beef stock and spice mixes, mix all of it up, then set it to simmer for 2 hours. If you were drinking a dark beer while cooking, pour that in now too.|Add the kidney beans, then let cook for another two hours.|
